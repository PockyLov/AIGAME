# Game Feel Skill

Use this skill when changing movement, jumping, collision, death, restart speed, or enemy contact behavior.

## Checklist

- Keep keyboard input responsive.
- Prefer small numeric tuning over broad rewrites.
- Preserve coyote time or another forgiving jump window when possible.
- Preserve a clear difference between stomping an enemy and side-hit death.
- Restart quickly after failure, but keep the failure reason visible.
- Test movement on ground and in air.

## Done When

- Player can move left and right immediately.
- Player can jump, land, and recover control in the air.
- Side collisions still punish the player.
- Stomp interactions remain reachable and readable.
- No obvious console errors are introduced.

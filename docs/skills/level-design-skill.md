# Level Design Skill

Use this skill when adding or tuning platforms, gaps, collectibles, enemies, and exit gates.

## Checklist

- Level 1 should teach one idea at a time.
- Level 2 may introduce danger, but the safe route must remain obvious.
- Level 3 can combine jumps, gaps, and enemies without requiring precision play.
- Use collectibles as route markers.
- Put the exit gate in a visually clear final position.
- Avoid layouts, names, or patterns copied from protected commercial games.

## Done When

- Each level has a clear start, route, challenge, and finish.
- Players can recover from normal jump timing.
- Enemies and hazards are visible before they are dangerous.
- The difficulty curve is gentle.

How to Play

Start from the in-game menu, collect energy sparks, avoid patrol bot side hits, and reach each glowing exit gate.

Controls

Keyboard: Arrow keys or A/D to move, Space/W/Up to jump, P/Esc to pause, R to restart.

Touch: use the on-screen Left, Right, Jump, and Pause buttons on mobile browsers.

Known Limits

This is a static web playable. It has no accounts, saves, leaderboard, backend, payments, Android APK, or iOS build.
